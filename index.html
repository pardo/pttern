A static model in plan: rigid walls that slide in, slide along and twist; clamps that squeeze each pair of flaps; cords that pull with their tension at every bend; and both ends of each miter face and the plate edge as one-way contacts. It shows where the force goes and whether the frame stays square. It ignores friction and flex: the clamped miter faces really grip each other too, so the model errs on the safe side, and real parts give a little.
